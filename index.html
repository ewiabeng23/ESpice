Who we are

A taste of Cameroon, straight from the grill

ESpice brings the flavours of Cameroon to London: Tozo, peppered fish and whole grills cooked over real charcoal and finished with our special Cameroonian sauce. Every order is grilled fresh, at the heat you choose.

From Bamenda to London

It started with family

I grew up in Bamenda, where the smell of charcoal and spice meant good food and good company. I started cooking and grilling for family and friends, and every gathering turned into a feast. What began as cooking for the people I love became a passion. Now I want everyone to have a taste of Cameroon.

How we cook

Real charcoal, no shortcuts

Every skewer and every fish is cooked over real charcoal. The meat is cut thin and rubbed with our house spices, and the fish is scored, peppered and grilled whole until the skin crisps. It takes time, and you can taste it.

Our signature

The sauce that makes it Cameroonian

Our fish and platters come with a special Cameroonian sauce, made the way we make it back home. The recipe stays in the family, but we will tell you this much: it is bold, it is fresh, and once you have tried it you will ask for extra.

How to order online
